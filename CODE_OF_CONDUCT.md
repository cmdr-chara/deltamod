# Deltamod Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Deltamod Community to be welcoming, safe, and constructive for everyone participating in good faith. Contributors, users, and maintainers are expected to respect one another across issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and respectful of different experiences and viewpoints.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.
- Focus criticism on ideas and changes, not people.
- Credit the work and sources used in contributions.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop an unwanted interaction.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and make a good-faith effort to repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, or unwanted personal attention.
- Discriminatory, insulting, demeaning, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Impersonation, evasion of moderation, spam, or misleading attribution.
- Any behavior that threatens the safety or well-being of the community.

## Scope

This code applies in all Deltamod Community spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents publicly when doing so could expose private or sensitive information. Contact the maintainer privately using the contact methods listed on the [cmdr-chara GitHub profile](https://github.com/cmdr-chara). Include relevant links or records and describe the impact; remove credentials and unrelated personal data.

Maintainers will review reports as privately and promptly as practical. Depending on severity and history, responses may include clarification, a warning, temporary limits, removal of content, suspension, or a permanent ban. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not permitted.
