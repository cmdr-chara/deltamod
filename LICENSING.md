## Redistribution and communication checklist

Article 5 of EUPL-1.2 imposes obligations when covered copies or derivative works are distributed or communicated. In particular, a distributor should ensure that it:

- keeps intact copyright, patent, trademark, licence, and warranty-disclaimer notices;
- includes the applicable notices and a copy of the EUPL with distributed or communicated copies;
- places prominent notices on derivative works stating that the work was modified and the date of modification;
- provides a machine-readable copy of the corresponding source code, or identifies a repository where that source remains easily and freely available for as long as the distributor continues to distribute or communicate the covered work;
- distributes covered original or derivative work only under licensing terms permitted by the EUPL, including its copyleft and compatibility clauses; and
- does not impose additional terms or conditions on EUPL-covered work that alter or restrict the rights granted by the EUPL.

The EUPL definition of **Distribution or Communication** is broad and includes making copies available online or offline and providing access to essential functionality to another natural or legal person. Hosted or service-based uses should therefore be reviewed against the actual EUPL text rather than assuming that source obligations apply only to downloadable binaries.

## Copyleft and compatible licences

EUPL-1.2 is a copyleft licence. Covered derivative works that are distributed or communicated remain subject to the EUPL's copyleft rules unless the EUPL compatibility clause permits distribution of a combined derivative work under a licence listed as compatible in the EUPL appendix.

The compatibility clause is part of the EUPL itself. This repository does not create a separate relicensing permission or an additional compatibility list.

## Contributions

Unless maintainers explicitly agree otherwise for separately licensed material before accepting it, a contribution submitted for inclusion in Deltamod Community is expected to be licensable and distributable under **EUPL-1.2 on the same terms as the repository's covered work**.

By submitting a contribution for inclusion, the contributor represents that they own the copyright in the contribution or otherwise have sufficient rights to provide it under those terms. Contributors retain their copyright; submitting a contribution does **not** assign copyright ownership to Deltamod Community or its maintainers.

Do not submit copied code, assets, generated material, or other content whose licence is incompatible, unclear, or cannot be satisfied by this repository. If a contribution contains separately licensed material, identify it clearly and preserve every required notice.

See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for the contribution workflow.

Third-party names, logos, game marks, service marks, and branding remain subject to their owners' rights. Open-source availability of the software does not by itself authorize confusing, misleading, or endorsement-implying use of branding.

## Termination on breach

Article 12 of EUPL-1.2 provides for automatic termination of the licence and granted rights upon breach by a licensee. Anyone redistributing or communicating covered work should resolve compliance issues before continuing to exercise rights that depend on the licence.

This section is only a pointer to the EUPL's termination rule; it does not add a new termination condition, cure period, penalty, or enforcement mechanism.
